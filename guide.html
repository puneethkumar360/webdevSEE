How ReLoop works

A simple demo for buying and selling used items.

Buy an item

  1. Browse products and read their condition and description.
  2. Select Buy now and enter sample contact and collection/delivery details.
  3. Open My purchases to edit details, mark received, or cancel before receipt.

Sell an item

Select Sell, add an honest description and price, then publish. Use My listings to edit, delete, mark sold, or relist it.

Trade carefully

  • Meet in a busy public place.
  • Inspect the item before paying.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs, or identity documents.
  • Avoid advance payments to strangers.

This is a browser-only demo. Data is saved with localStorage on this device. There are no real accounts, payments, deliveries, messages, or moderation services.
